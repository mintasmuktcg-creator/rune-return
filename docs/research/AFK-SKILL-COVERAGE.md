## Explicit limitations

- **rs3-dungeoneering:** Ordinary floors require active navigation and combat. Group leeching depends on other players and is not a solo AFK progression method. Seasonal activities are not assumed available year-round. [Source](https://runescape.wiki/w/AFK#Dungeoneering).

Batch = repeat production manually; passive = progress attached to another activity or a return-later resource; active = useful alternative that is not AFK; variable = interruptions depend on the setup; long = longer stretches with inventory/resource/logout limits.
