# Creator and community methods

Reviewed November 2, 2026. The registry contains **53 publications: 49 written
section reviews and four video chapter indexes**. See the [deep-dive report](AFK-DEEP-DIVE.md)
for findings, exclusions and remaining work, and the [source inventory](research/afk-source-inventory.csv)
for every captured/reference URL and its evidence state.

## Evidence in the application

Creator guides stay collapsed inside the selected skill. Publication, revision
and our check dates have separate meanings. Unknown publication dates stay
unknown; a recording date is not substituted for a publication date.

Written section reviews mean the relevant published explanation was examined.
They do not claim personal gameplay testing or independent measurements.
Link'sOcarina, Qp RS, Kaoz OSRS and Silenced video description/chapter indexes
support discovery only; gameplay, equipment and numeric claims remain unreviewed.

The **Creator/community reviewed** filter uses written method associations.
Chapter-only links do not qualify. Sources needing compatibility review can
appear with warnings without endorsing their methods. Current patch evidence
resolves stale tiers and unlock conflicts; a newer title alone does not.

The catalog has **205 entries**, with **86 creator-linked entries**. Multiple
tiers are not distinct activities or independent sources. Each route retains
a mechanical reference. The skill coverage matrix exposes missing associations.

## Rates and compatibility

No universal XP/hour, expected rare-drop profit or safe unattended combat claim
is inferred from a creator title. Reported durations remain conditional on gear,
storage, staffing, supplies and client timers. Historical prices are not current
GP estimates. No article body, transcript, video or thumbnail is rehosted; the
app uses outbound links instead of embedded players.

The mouse-movement/combat-idle disagreement remains unresolved by our own tests;
no mouse-only reset instruction was imported. The deep dive also found incorrect
Hunter/eel levels, stale RS3 Fletching tiers and publisher compatibility warnings.
See the report for the decisions and original sources.

## Maintenance

The registry and generated reviewed metadata use stable IDs. Explicit decisions
live in research/afk-reviewed-publications.json; captures alone never qualify.
The research scripts need private local captures, but normal builds do not.

Before associating a method, check levels, quests, access, account mode, gear,
supplies, whole-loop attention, rate exclusions and patch compatibility. Budget
and maximum-gear setups need different evidence. A partial style update cannot
clear an entire publisher warning.

The full automated suite has **69 tests**. Browser checks cover skill selection,
source states, persistent filters, patch links and responsive layouts. Gameplay
verification and automatic creator-feed monitoring are not configured.
